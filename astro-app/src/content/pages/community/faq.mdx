## ❓ Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

### About KASH TOKEN Fundamentals

Q1: What is KASH TOKEN?

A: KASH TOKEN (KASH) is a fixed-supply, utility token built on the Binance Smart Chain (BSC). Its primary purpose is to be the core currency and economic engine for the two external utility platforms funded by the KASH Foundation: KASH 1 (E-commerce) and KASH 2 (Trade & Investment).

Q2: Why is KASH TOKEN a utility token?

A: KASH is designed exclusively to grant access to and act as the payment/fee mechanism within our ecosystem's services. It is not a security token and does not represent ownership or equity in a company. Its value is tied entirely to its real-world utility and adoption across the KASH 1 and KASH 2 platforms.

Q3: What is the total supply of KASH?

A: The total and maximum supply is fixed at 10,000,000,000 KASH. The contract has no minting function, meaning no new tokens can ever be created, guaranteeing scarcity.

### Tokenomics and Fees

Q4: What are the transaction fees?

A: KASH TOKEN features a revolutionary 0% owner-imposed transaction fee. There are no buy taxes, sell taxes, or reflection fees embedded in the smart contract. Users only pay the minimal BNB Gas Fee required by the BSC network to execute the transaction.

Q5: What is the vesting schedule for the Team and Advisors?

A: Zero-Vesting, Zero-Lock. All tokens, including the 10% allocated to the Team and Advisors, are liquid immediately upon deployment. This ensures absolute transparency and aligns the team's long-term success with the community's success.

Q6: How are the Foundation funds secured?

A: The 30% KASH Foundation fund, dedicated to funding the KASH 1 and KASH 2 platforms, is secured in an auditable Multi-Signature (Multi-Sig) Wallet. Multiple team signatories must approve any transaction, ensuring community safety and eliminating any single point of failure.

### Technical Information

Q7: What blockchain does KASH operate on?

A: KASH TOKEN operates on the Binance Smart Chain (BSC). The token standard is BEP-20 (a version of the ERC-20 standard).

Q8: Why did you choose the Binance Smart Chain (BSC)?

A: BSC was chosen for its optimal balance of low transaction costs (gas fees) and high transaction speed, which is essential for a currency designed for high-volume, small-scale commerce (KASH 1).
