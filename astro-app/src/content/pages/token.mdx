KASH Token: Freedom in Every Transaction

KASH is a utility token designed for micro‑transactions, tipping, and community empowerment. Built on a fixed supply with zero owner fees, it ensures fairness and transparency. Unlike speculative tokens, KASH is focused on real‑world use cases:

- Everyday payments without barriers
- Peer‑to‑peer transfers that are instant and secure
- Community swaps for real‑world assets

KASH is not about staking, burning, or hidden costs — it’s about freedom, accessibility, and trust.
