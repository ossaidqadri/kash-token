## The KASH Roadmap in Brief

The KASH TOKEN roadmap is focused entirely on delivering real-world utility and achieving our $\\$0.01$ valuation target by Year 4.

The first phase (Year 1) is dedicated to establishing the core infrastructure: launching the KASH Token on the Binance Smart Chain (BSC), completing the third-party security audit, and immediately activating the Multi-Sig Foundation Wallet to begin funding for our utility projects. We will also introduce simple token utility through tipping and initial NFT marketplace integration. The second phase (Year 2) then shifts to development and market access, focusing on the Alpha and Beta releases of our KASH 1 E-commerce Marketplace and the concept development of the KASH 2 Trade & Investment Platform, alongside securing our first Centralized Exchange (CEX) listings.

The subsequent phases (Years 3 and 4) are dedicated to adoption and growth. In Year 3, we execute the full global launch of KASH 1, initiating aggressive merchant acquisition drives funded by the KASH Foundation to cement KASH as the preferred low-fee e-commerce currency. We also roll out the KASH DAO Governance system for community oversight and launch the Beta for the KASH 2 RWA (Real-World Asset) Swap platform. Finally, Year 4 focuses on market maturity, achieving a Tier 1 CEX listing, and the full launch of the KASH 2 platform to unlock advanced trade and micro-investment utility, which together are designed to secure and sustain our target valuation.
